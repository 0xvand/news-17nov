# Sources — the reporting behind every item on the front page

All stories were reported on **Tuesday 17 November 2026**. Each item below
was cross-checked in at least two independent reports before it went on
the page.

## Lead — U.S. strikes Iran's Qeshm Island; tankers burn in Hormuz
U.S. warplanes struck a missile launch site on Iran's Qeshm Island in
retaliation for Iranian attacks on U.S. personnel and assets; a supertanker
and a gas carrier were struck by naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, and
Oman evacuated 24 seafarers from the burning gas carrier Tara Gas. Iran's
IRGC said it struck a U.S. radar system at Bahrain's Sheikh Isa base.

## Europe — Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge again
Ukrainian drones and missiles struck the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge again on
Tuesday; Russia answered with a major ballistic missile attack on Kyiv
that hit bridges over the Dnipro and disrupted heating and water.

## Diplomacy — Trump, Xi and Putin converge on Shenzhen
Leaders gathered in Shenzhen, China, for the APEC summit, where U.S.

Thursday.

## Gaza — West urges Israel to open Gaza crossings
The United Kingdom, Australia, Canada and Italy issued a joint statement
calling on Israel to allow rapid humanitarian access into Gaza, calling
the situation catastrophic; the UN warned that winter rains are flooding
makeshift shelters and worsening the shelter and food crisis.

## Climate — COP31 draft urges fossil-fuel phase-out
Ministers took over the COP31 talks in Antalya as the first draft of the
cover decision — published by the Turkish and Australian presidencies —
proposed accelerated action to phase out fossil fuels and tripling
adaptation finance to at least $120 billion a year by 2035.

## Tech — U.S. tightens AI chip rules on China
The Trump administration issued rules requiring export licences for
advanced AI chips and servers used in overseas data centres, aimed at
stopping Chinese firms from accessing them remotely; China's Foreign
Ministry criticised the curbs.

## Health — U.S. loses its measles elimination status
The Pan American Health Organization announced that the United States
had lost its measles elimination status after evidence of uninterrupted
transmission for a year, with the highest case counts in more than three
decades; Mexico and Bolivia also lost the designation.

## Markets ticker — Wall Street falls; Nvidia beats forecasts after the bell
Major U.S. indexes closed lower on Tuesday under pressure from AI and
technology stocks (S&P 500 −1.0%, Dow −1.6%, Nasdaq −0.7%); after the
close, Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.84 billion, above
expectations, and its shares rose about 4%.
